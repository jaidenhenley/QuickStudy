# QuickStudy · Japanese (ja) glossary

Voice: です・ます, warm and short, like Apple's own Japanese UI. Buttons are verb phrases without
です・ます (「カードを保存」, 「再試行」), matching iOS. Full-width 、。「」？！（）： in sentences; no
spaces between Japanese words. Numbers take counters with no space (12枚, 3ページ, 5分). Titles of
user content (set names, document names) are quoted in 「」 inside sentences. Middle dot separators
stay as " · ". Japanese has no plural forms, so every plural key is a plain string.

Never translated: QuickStudy, QuickStudy Pro, Pro, iPhone, Apple Intelligence, Private Cloud Compute,
Claude, Anthropic, PDF, API. Possessives are written 「AppleのPrivate Cloud Compute」,
「AnthropicのClaude」.
